# Fire staff — generated-target redo
Target: ai-target/concept.png, built-in image generation. v1 rejected because TOP was an elevation; corrected with an axial overhead edit. No construction diagram used as visual target. Source: weapons-v1 row 2 column 2 and fire-staff YAML. Target shows some view-dependent prong positions; preserve three-prong construction and warm flame identity.

## Round 1 — blockout (3)
Opened comparison.png: length and head/haft ratio are close. Rectangular head lacks pointed curl and belly; collar is a solid shelf instead of an open cradle. Thin plain shaft lacks wrapped grip and heavy pommel. All seven requested views plus both left hands fit. Next: stepped flame silhouette, open angled prongs, collars, grip and pommel.

## Round 2 — shapes (28)
Opened comparison: flame now tapers and gold forks enclose negative space. Front matches the asymmetric belly; side is a little narrow and the top footprint is more rectangular than the reference's faceted triangle. Handle remains visibly straight and smooth. Next: raised diagonal wood ridges, leather wrap, collar corner bevels, two-sided flame core and socket/pommel rubies.

## Round 3 — details (101)
Opened comparison: warm gold/orange identity reads at 32px; ruby socket and pommel now anchor the staff. Core is visible front and iso, but side is predominantly orange without bright facets; silhouette still has an overly straight shaft. Flat material colors hide the wrap relief. Next: material ramps/grain, incised prong ridges and socket lips; slightly strengthen shaft width to match the concept.

## Round 4 — texture (106)
Opened comparison: gold fittings and leather wrap have readable highlights; orange flame has become too yellow because short UV patches start on highlight texels. Spiral relief is too quiet against the brown shaft. Top remains a stepped footprint; it is less triangular than the AI target. Next: shift energy-shell sampling into red-orange midtones, brighten raised wood ridges, add curling point and sparse flame facet glints.

## Round 5 — polish (113)
Opened final comparison: orange-red outer flame and yellow core are distinct again, curl tip is clearer, wood spiral is now visible in front and iso. Gold cradle has open negative space in front and is volumetric in side/top. 16px preserves warm head/wood shaft, 32px separates prongs and pommel; tiny ruby details require 64px. Both hand transform previews fit. Local visual verdict: accepted as a cuboid interpretation. Weaknesses: top silhouette is squarer than target, prong layout is not an exact triangular replica, flame is opaque/static, and stepped facets cannot reproduce the painting's triangular surfaces. Actual client hand pose remains unverified.
