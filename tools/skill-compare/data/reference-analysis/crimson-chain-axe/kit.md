Sinclair, 3-star, World of Grand Magasin Sisyphe, Season 8 PUNCTUM, 2026.10.15. Traits Sisyphe, Le Rouge. HP 266, Def 65, Speed 3~6 -> 4~7 at T3, Stagger 55% (146). Page is huge; tier labels unclear. Skills have many selectable variants (Pierce all). Skills list "SP" cost shown on page (5/4/3/7 etc.) - SP is wiki shorthand as given; per-coin numbers below.
Skill 1 (variants): 
- "La Pulsion" (Gluttony): Pierce 5 (T4) +6, 1 coin, weight 66 (65+1); can target allies (Duality); T2 Clash Power +1 per 4 Bleed (max 2); T4 Atk Weight + (Pulsation-1) max 2, sub-targets Le Rouge allies by ascending HP%. Coin: at Attack End on ally, 2x/turn heal (10+Pulsation)% HP (max 15%) and SP (max 15).
- "Je vous aide" (Pride): Pierce 5 +6, 1 coin, weight 66; Unclashable; Final Power +1 per 4 Bleed (max 4); +5% dmg per 1% target missing HP (max 100%); On Hit once/turn 1 Lust Fragility (+(X*10)% Lust dmg, max 100%).
- "Je m'expulse" (Gluttony): Pierce 3, 3 coins, weight 68 (65+3); Noble Dignity, Pulsation gain, L'Abject (1 -> 2 at T4); +1 Coin Power if 6+ Bleed (T4). 
- "Le Trait unaire" (Lust, Skill 1 variant, T3 unlock): Pierce 5 +4, 2 coins, weight 67 (65+2), Clashable Counter-type; 3+/5+ Pulsation Coin Power +1/+2. C1 1 Bleed + 1 L'Abject; C2 same, applies Uncontrollable Rampage if active.
Skill 2:
- "L'Appétit" (Wrath): Pierce 4 +5, 2 coins, weight 67 (65+2); T4 Atk Weight +1 per 2 Pulsation (max 2); ally targeting; applies 1 Clash Power Up + Desperate Hunger to allies; Bleed on hit.
- "Je m'abjecte" (Wrath, T3): Pierce 4 +4/+5, 3 coins, weight 69 (65+4); consumes HP from two other allies pre-attack; Bleed, L'Abject, Lust Fragility on hit; +1 Coin Power if 6+ Bleed.
- "La Demande" (Pride): Pierce 5 +5, 2 coins, weight 68 (65+3); Unclashable; scales with Bleed; removes L'Abject, inflicts Lust Fragility (1 -> 2 at T4); Pulsation 3+ Coin Power +1. (Wiki labels it Skill 3 in tier notes.)
Skill 3:
- "Le Baptême rouge" (Lust): Pierce 4 +3, 3 coins, weight 68 (65+3); Combat Start consumes ally HP for Offense Level/Attack Power Up; Final Power +1 per 4 Bleed (max 2); each coin On Hit +3 Relentless Hunger.
- "Le Manque-à-être" (Gluttony): Pierce 8, 1 coin, weight 68; Unclashable, Target Fixed; recovers ally from Stagger, heals target 15% max HP; gain 5 Bind.
- "La Voracité" (Lust): Pierce 3, 4 coins, weight 70 (65+5); replaced by La Castration at 150 Relentless Hunger; consumes ally HP; +1 Final Power per 4 Bleed (max 2).
- "La Castration" (Lust, T3 lock): Pierce 4 +3, 5 Unbreakable Coins, weight 70 (65+5). C1 +2 Bleed Count +1 L'Abject (+1 more on main target). C2 1 Bleed 1 L'Abject. C3 2 Bleed. C4 activate Bleed once, -1 Bleed Count (variant: removes all L'Abject, Lust Fragility). C5 consume up to 15 Pulsation, +(consumed x3)% dmg, activate Bleed. Combat Start consumes 50% current HP of other Le Rouge allies -> 3 Uncontrollable Rampage. Attack End consumes all Relentless Hunger.

"La Garde" (Gluttony, Guard 2 +7, 1 Unbreakable coin, weight 65): Clash Win raise target Stagger Threshold by Clash Power; Clash Lose reduce attacker Final Power; when hit +2 Relentless Hunger; Final Power +1 per 4 Bleed (max 3).

"La Jouissance phallique": +1 Pulsation, Pulsation per Bleed activation (max 10/turn); at Combat End 15+ Pulsation grants Rampage and heals. "Les Pouvoirs de la vie" (2 Lust, 2 Gluttony): Noble Dignity by Pulsation (2+: -25% Bleed dmg; 3+: Base Skills +1 Bleed Potency/Count; 4+: Base Power +1); uses Le Manque-à-être on staggered allies. Support "Voracious Overindulgence": Combat Start 2 Offense Level Up to lowest-HP ally, heals 5% at Attack Skill End.
