## What the previous analyses got wrong

1. **Invented Don lance/spear/rapier.** Clear guard and extended-hand frames do not show a persistent shaft, hilt or blade. Thin orange/cyan strike lines are transient VFX. S2 visibly includes an inverted foot strike. Do not equip her with a trident based on these frames.
2. **Merged and split Don's skills at arbitrary times.** The first old analysis put the next skill's opening and kick inside “DQ-1” and isolated S2's last rush as “DQ-2”. Native-frame neutral resets at 14.500 and 17.833 establish the three separate blocks.
3. **Treated every flare, ring and hold as another hit.** Don's final target push and Gregor's repeated-looking held finisher do not automatically add coins. Four Don S3 groups are a kit-supported interpretation, not proof of four UI damage ticks; competing C4 onset is retained.
4. **Merged Gregor's 39 s and 43 s attacks.** A visible neutral reset at 41.933 separates them. The 39 s jet sequence is likely an S1 animation variant; the 43 s double punch is the better S2 candidate. Variant cause remains unknown.
5. **Misidentified the checkerboard clash passage as S2/ring-cannon damage.** Three white crossing clash flashes accompany enemy re-approaches; a unilateral sweeping counter follows. These flashes should not become three extra outgoing damage coins.
6. **Called a small gauntlet ignition a 3-body-height floor column, and enlarged arcs using zoomed screen size as world size.** The S3 ignition is roughly 0.3–0.7 H. Camera crops make the later flames fill the screen.
7. **Said each fiery hoop is a projectile/hit.** The two dominant finisher hoops are pressure effects near the thrust; target knockback changes separation. No independent collision per hoop is visible.
8. **Missed exact inserts.** Don has two dark frames at 22.167/22.200. Gregor has a one-frame mechanism close-up at 56.900, two black frames at 56.933/56.967, then a yellow-filled flame close-up; this is not simply a two-tick yellow flash.
9. **Claimed enemies collapse and silence follows.** The blue-hall target remains upright in 58.867–60.500. This video-only review does not infer silence from images.
10. **Invented mechanics for the Minecraft translation.** Don Charge resource, overheat lockout, per-tick jet damage, per-ring hits and consuming target Burn were not supported by the provided kit. Preserve the supplied Poise/Nerve Strike/Fuel distinctions; do not derive them from colours alone. Orange streaks alone also do not prove a critical hit.

## Reproduction and limits

Each skill JSON contains weapon, props, caster motion, candidate coin contacts, beats, effects, cameras and sheet references. Continuous flame jets use `beam` because the requested range enum has no flame-stream value. Body distances and colour hexes are approximate visual estimates. Combat contact evidence is native 30 fps; no interpolation was used. The actual damage frame can be hidden by flare, cropping, hit-stop or edits, so uncertainty is recorded rather than manufacturing one-frame precision. Intro/outro montage boundaries are coarser (6 fps, up to five frames). No independent summoned weapons, projectiles or clones were confirmed. Hand echoes and pressure loops are explicitly VFX.
