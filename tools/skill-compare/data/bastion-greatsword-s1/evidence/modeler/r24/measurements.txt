# r24 modeler: rig blade darker than the held item from below (vfx-reviewer r22 #5, animator r23 opponent t10-11). ADR-0253.
# Luminance = Rec.709 Y of 1280x720 real-client PNGs (worktree build, one capture at a time, maxRepeatedFrames 1 in all takes).

Cause (26.3 client jar, javap):
  block displays and held items use the same item_cutout pipeline / core/item.vsh; the vertex normal is the quad's face
  direction through the pose normal matrix; brightness = min(1, 0.6*(max(0,n.L0)+max(0,n.L1)) + 0.4),
  L0/L1 = normalize(0.2,1,-0.7) / normalize(-0.2,1,0.7) fixed in the world. Down face 0.40, side faces 0.40-0.75
  (azimuth mean 0.62), up 1.0. `shade: false` only feeds BlockModelLighter (chunk meshing). Light level not involved
  (rig rides the caster, sky 15 in daylight). So the rig was lit exactly like a held item would be in that pose; a
  swing just shows faces the held item never shows.
  Contact pose (key 6, opponent t8-10): the broad face seen from below has n = (0.46, -0.75, 0.48) -> 0.40.
  Held pose (key 0, caster yaw 0): broad faces n = (+-1, 0, 0) -> 0.50; the edge facing the opponent n.y = -0.62 -> 0.40.
  face_normals.py prints all faces per key with vanilla and the final lift.

  rig underside after 0.61-0.62 x texture. Held broad face (vertical) is 0.40-0.75 by azimuth: 0.50 at the opponent take's

  rule on the rig alone (v1: hand-over pose has faces 38-52 deg below horizontal, the contact face 49 deg: no threshold separates them).
