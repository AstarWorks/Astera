# ADR-0300: Placed custom block look

## Status

Accepted (MODE-ARS7, founder delegation 2026-10-28).

## Context

The founder requires every authored cover block to be uniquely identifiable in the world, held and dropped.
Existing material-only reverse lookup confuses terrain with cover and vanilla item placement bypasses the
custom gateway. There are nine cover definitions and four ore-node definitions; ore nodes stay vanilla.
Paper 26.3 is the target. No Mojang PNG derivatives or paid asset generation are permitted.

## Decision

Reserve `minecraft:note_block[instrument=harp,note=1..9,powered=false]`. YAML `visual` declares the
complete state and `texture` its original 32×32 surface. One pack-generated `cube_all` model serves both
that world state and the `placed_from` item definition. Paper placement routes the actual materialized
item through the existing temporary-block gateway, preserving vanilla placement checks and consumption.
Full state, never the underlying vanilla cover material, identifies visual-backed cover for HP and drops.
All note-block tuning, sound, redstone and physics are suppressed, including ordinary notes, to prevent
vanilla tuning from entering a reservation. Note=0 remains the vanilla placement state; note blocks do
not occur in terrain world generation. The lint rejects note=0, other instruments/power states, missing
placement visuals and duplicate state claims. Operator commands can deliberately author reserved states.

| Block | note | HP | Surface |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| barricade | 1 | 300 | crossed amber beams, iron band |
| hardened-soil | 2 | 500 | compacted earth layers, stamp |
| packed-clay | 3 | 350 | terracotta tiles, square seal |
| reinforced-planks | 4 | 450 | olive timber, two steel bands |
| reinforced-wall | 5 | 400 | brick and riveted steel plate |
| sandbags | 6 | 200 | staggered stitched sack rows |
| stone-bulwark | 7 | 600 | blue-grey ashlar, pale marker stripe |
| tempered-glass | 8 | 250 | cyan pane reflection, dark frame |
| obsidian-plate | 9 | 1500 | charcoal plates, violet seams |

Every surface has the same pale corner bracket and dark rivet. Original procedural art is declared in
`content/textures/sources/placed-blocks.yaml` and reproducibly generated by `tools/item-art` (`blocks content`
for an isolated run). No existing `content/models` file is edited: cube models are assembled in the pack.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The state itself survives chunk save/load and identifies durability without a position-identity database.
- All break sources using the HP gateway drop the original Astera item via the shared item materializer.
- Plain dirt, stone, planks and glass no longer silently gain custom HP or custom drops.
- Hand mining uses the existing six-tick custom HP swing model instead of the carrier's wood hardness.

### Negative / Trade-offs

- Vanilla note-block music is unavailable while this listener is installed. This is intentional for the
  current arena product; a future music feature must use a different carrier or explicitly unreserve states.
- Note blocks are opaque full cubes. Tempered glass therefore uses the task's allowed **opaque framed-glass**
  alternative; it no longer provides a view through cover. The item matches the placed surface exactly.
- Paper physics may only emit a root event. The listener checks six adjacent carrier candidates and
  recovers the canonical state by the invariant reserved note (shape updates may have already changed the
  instrument), then schedules restoration of surviving same-note carriers after propagation. This costs six material reads
  per physics event and one scoped callback per adjacent carrier. Direct note physics is cancelled immediately;
  redstone and playback have separate guards. Pistons cannot move carriers.
- Legacy map structures authored as vanilla mangrove planks/deepslate tiles/packed mud/rooted dirt are no
  longer custom cover merely because their material matches. Their owner must migrate intended cover cells
  to the declared states; terrain must stay vanilla.

### Mitigations

- Full-state shipped-pack integration coverage checks every mapping, distinct texture and shared cube model.
- `CustomBlockLookE2eTest` checks neighbor/power updates, exact authored hit counts and item components on
  real Paper. `placed-block-shot.sh` exercises actual client item placement in an ACTIVE PvE match and
  captures the row with vanilla contrast blocks at 3 m and 8 m under the host-wide shot lock.
- The pack retains vanilla note-block models for every unreserved state. Lint and the pack builder both
  reject duplicate/unsafe claims. Warm pack rebuild inputs include `content/blocks`.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Mushroom face combinations**: adjacent placement/removal mutates faces and world generation already
  uses many combinations. Collision suppression and authored-state auditing are less straightforward.
- **Persistent leaves / glass panes**: useful transparency but leaf tint/decay and pane connections add
  carrier-specific behavior and collision geometry. A separate transparent carrier for one block is not
  justified for this iteration; the explicitly permitted opaque glass direction keeps one mechanism.
- **Display entities per cell**: separate lifecycle, lighting, collision and repair identity for every block.
