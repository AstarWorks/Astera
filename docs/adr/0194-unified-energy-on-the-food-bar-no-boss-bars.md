# ADR-0194: One energy resource on the food bar, ammo on the status line, no boss bars

## Status

Accepted. Replaces the boss-bar row (and the "mana bar is the boss bar" sentence) of
ADR-0177 section 3; every other part of ADR-0177 (one owner per channel, action-bar
priority, sidebars) and ADR-0182 (resource pack, HP status line) stands. Narrows
ADR-0080 section 4 (the Phase 1 resource catalogue is now `Ammo` + `Energy`).

## Context

Founder: "Stamina = the hunger bar. Make mana and stamina the same thing, shown in the
HUD. Don't use boss bars."

WP2 / WP14 gave each weapon its own pool (ammo for guns, stamina for blades, mana for
staves) and drew the held item's pool as a boss bar (slot 1). Three pools and a bar at the
top of the screen made swapping weapons feel like swapping characters, and the boss bar
sat where Minecraft draws bosses. The vanilla food bar was unused but still live: hunger
drained, saturation healed, starvation hurt.

## Decision

### 1. One resource: `energy`

`ResourceType` is now `Ammo | Energy`. `Stamina` and `Mana` are gone everywhere
(pre-release: renamed in one sweep, no alias; `ResourceType.fromKey("mana")` is `null`).

- `PlayerResourcePool(ammo, energy)`; every melee swing, dash and spell spends `energy`.
- `content/items/*.yaml`: `resource_type: energy` for blades and staves. They no longer
  set `resource_max_pool` / `resource_regen_per_second`: the cap and the regen are global
  (`ResourceCap.DEFAULTS`: **100 max, 15 per second**; was 100 / 20 for stamina and 60-100 /
  6 for mana), so swapping weapons never changes the pool. Guns keep their magazine size.
- `content/skills/*.yaml`: `resource_type: energy` (or omitted = the item's), costs unchanged.
  Roster feel: a staff caster used to regenerate 6/s, now 15/s, a blade user 20/s, now 15/s;
  the shared pool is drawn down by whichever weapon is held.
- `CastRejectionReason.INSUFFICIENT_MANA` / `INSUFFICIENT_STAMINA` became
  `INSUFFICIENT_ENERGY` (`astera.combat.cast.insufficient.energy`).
- `AdminItemCatalog` told swords from staves by `Stamina` / `Mana`; it now uses the vanilla
  material (`*_SWORD` / `*_AXE` = blade, else rod / staff).

### 2. Energy is the vanilla food bar

`EnergyFoodBarListener` (core, the bar's one owner) writes
`foodLevel = EnergyFoodBar.foodLevelFor(energy, max)` (0..20) through
`IPlayerFoodGateway.mirrorEnergy`:

- 0 only at energy 0, 20 only at full, anything between reads 1..19 (rounded);
- triggers: `ResourceSpent` / `ResourceRegenned` of energy, `PlayerActiveWeaponChanged`
  (the cap can differ), join, respawn, plus a 1 s resync poll (vanilla resets food on
  respawn and other plugins can write it; the gateway compares with the real value).

**Vanilla hunger is neutralised** in `PaperPlayerFoodGateway`:

| Vanilla effect | Handling |
|---|---|
| exhaustion / eating / starvation tick changing food | `FoodLevelChangeEvent` cancelled for **everyone**; Astera's own writes use `Player.setFoodLevel`, which fires no such event, so they pass |
| saturation | zeroed on every mirror write (and in the cancel handler), so the bar never "holds" |
| exhaustion | zeroed on every mirror write |
| natural regeneration from satiety | `EntityRegainHealthEvent` with `SATIATED` / `REGEN` cancelled (HP is the Astera container, ADR-0149); other reasons untouched |
| starvation damage at food 0 (energy 0) | `EntityDamageEvent` with cause `STARVATION` cancelled |

`IPlayerFoodGateway` lost `suppressHungerHud` / `isHungerSuppressed` / `setHungerAsMana`
(nothing called them in production) and gained `mirrorEnergy`. `PlayerFoodChanged` lost the
`suppressed` flag.

**Sprinting.** Minecraft lets a player *start* a sprint only above food level 6, i.e. above
30 % energy (`EnergyFoodBar.SPRINT_THRESHOLD_RATIO`). We deliberately keep that: sprinting
costs no energy, but running on fumes (energy at or below 30 %) is not allowed until it
regenerates. Chosen because it needs no client trick (the client decides), it reads as
"exhausted" next to the visible bar, and 15/s regen restores it in about two seconds. If
playtests say it is annoying, the knob is a different mapping in `EnergyFoodBar` (e.g.
floor the level at 7 while energy > 0) - one function, no other change.

### 3. Ammo on the status line, no boss bar

`HpStatusHudService` (the status line's one owner, ADR-0182) also draws the ammo of the held
gun: with the resource pack loaded, `[bullet] cur/max` in the status atlas digits, right of
the HP bar (new glyph `StatusHudGlyphs.AMMO`, U+E10B, generated by `generateHudArt`;
`AmmoReadoutLayout` joins it to the HP block with an 8 px gap and one `FontText` width, so the
gateway's anchoring still lands the whole block on the heart row); without the pack, the text
`HP cur/max  Ammo cur/max` (`astera.hud.status.hp-ammo`). It is polled with the HP line (every
2 ticks), so a shot shows within 0.1 s, and it disappears when the held item has no ammo. The
status line was chosen over the item name / count because the line already exists, survives
toasts, needs no item-meta rewrite per shot (which resyncs the slot and resets use animations),
and puts HP and ammo in the same glance.

### 4. Reload progress is the item cooldown sweep

`ResourceReloadStarted` -> `ReloadCooldownOverlayListener` ->
`IItemCooldownGateway.showHeldItemCooldown(player, reloadTicks)`; the Paper adapter calls
`Player.setCooldown(heldStack, ticks)`, which the client draws as the native sweep over the
item. (`InMemoryItemCooldownGateway` records sweeps for the simulator.)

### 5. Boss bars are removed from the HUD

Removed: `HudUpdateOnResourcePoolChangedListener`, `HudLayout.BossBarSlot`,
`BossBarColor` / `BossBarStyle`, `HudRegion.BOSS_BAR_SLOT_1..4`, the `bossBar` DSL call, the
`ManaBarHud` example and `content/hud/mana-bar.json` + `content/hud/mana/*`, the gateway's
boss-bar branch, and - since nothing else used them - the whole `IBossBar` /
`IBossBarManager` abstraction with its Paper, in-memory and fake implementations and the
`hud.bossbar.match.*` language keys. `HudDiagnosticsService` keeps the `bossbar=` field of
the `HUD channels` log line (constant `none`) so the line stays comparable and a regression
is visible.

### Simulator

`SimHudRecorder.statusAmmo(player)` reads the ammo from the status line (glyph or text form);
`GameSim.foodLevel(entity)` and `cooldownSweeps()` expose the food bar and the reload sweeps;
`bossBarLayoutsRecorded` counts boss-bar regions ever recorded (the type no longer exists, so
it is always 0).

## Consequences

- One number to manage in the HUD: HP bar + ammo on the status line, energy on the food bar.
- Balance moved: staff casters regenerate faster, blade users slower; the TTK / roster
  scenario tests stay green with the unchanged skill costs, but a playtest pass over
  cost / regen (`ResourceCap.DEFAULTS`) is due.
- Vanilla food, eating and satiety healing are gone for all players, in every world on the
  Paper backends (Astera has no eating loop; ADR-0097).
- Needs a client pass: the bullet glyph and the combined block position (the HP block is now
  wider while a gun is held, so the anchor math runs on the combined width), the cooldown sweep
  on the held stack, and sprint behaviour below 30 % energy.
