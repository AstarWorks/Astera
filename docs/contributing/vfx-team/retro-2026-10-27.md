# Retrospective 2026-10-27: why rounds regress (bastion S3 r1-r6, couture S2 r1-r5)

Scope: the Limbus skill-reproduction workflow (v3, [README.md](README.md)) as run on bastion-greatsword S3 (6 rounds, no PASS) and couture-rapier S2. The S1 retrospectives fixed *who builds* (one author) and *what is written first* (the beat sheet). This one is about *how a round is judged*: every failure below is a case where someone judged without ground truth, or nobody judged at all.

Outcome: the countermeasures are in the role docs, the agent files, the round template ([round-template.md](round-template.md)) and the `make round-gate` check. Tooling requests that are outside this change are listed at the end.

## Top 5 root causes (what to remember)

1. **No ground truth.** Timing and distance were judged by eye from captures whose own labelling and scene were unverified. Fix: trace numbers are mandatory; eye measurement is never a reason to change content.
2. **No regression baseline.** Rounds were judged in isolation; "all rows ○" and "worse than last round" were both true at once. Fix: `ref | this | previous` per round, "weaker than previous: none" is a gate.
3. **Review surface ≠ reference framing.** Far wide-angle side views and a too-close preview were compared with a close cinematic reference. Fix: framing parity (caster bbox tolerance) is a validity condition of a capture.
4. **Tool fidelity assumed.** Nobody checked that the capture / preview could show the effect being judged. Fix: per-mechanic fidelity checklist in every round.
5. **Nobody with authority looked.** Author, reviewer and manager each had a reason not to look at the whole output. Fix: author self-look, reviewer whole-impression first, manager checklist, all enforced by the round gate.

## Reconciling "numbers are never acceptance evidence"

The S1 retrospective banned numbers because the author had been defining its own quality targets (luma, ink %, coverage) and passing them. That ban stays: **no self-authored quality target is a reason for ○**. It never meant "no facts": trace spawn ticks, caster positions, caster bbox share, the dev sha, the capture `skippedTicks` are measured by tools the author does not control, and they are now **mandatory**. The rule reads: *quality is judged by eye against the reference; facts (time, distance, framing, provenance) are read from the trace and the compare API, never by eye.*

## Addendum 2, same day: couture-rapier-s2 r6 reads as a test rig (「テストのための実装感がぬぐえない」)

Founder, on the skill-compare case: around t38 the thrown shears fly away from the mob; the "stuck on mob" and on-hit effects spawn at a fixed spot tuned to the capture camera whether or not anything was hit. The content and runtime fix is a separate director's job; this addendum is about why the workflow produced it and what now stops it.

Evidence (content at `8d86c9eac`, case data, git history):

- **Thrown weapon on keyframes.** `content/vfx/couture-s2-rig.yaml` (`follow: true`, `attach_to_anchor: true`, `orient: yaw`) carries `fly-shears`, an `item` element riding caster-local keyframes from (0, 1.5, 0.6) to (0, 1.3, 5.0) over t22-46, and `linked-shears` parked at caster-local (0, 1.9, 3.4) for t46-58. Nothing in that path reads the enemy: the shears fly to z 5.0 because the dummy stood at `DISTANCE 5.4`. The engine's `projectile` mechanic (`visual.display`, `hit_entity:` / `hit_block:` / `miss:` / `expire:` phases, used by 32 skills) was not used.
- **Hit effects tick-timed and decoupled from damage.** `content/skills/couture-rapier-s2.yaml` plays `couture-s2-hit1` / `hit2` / `hit3` as cast-time `play_vfx` steps at `delay_ticks` 8 / 38 / 72 with a 24-degree, 7-block cone targeter and `snapshot_pose: true`, while the `damage` steps with the same cone sit at 9 / 41 and the finisher is a `dash_strike` at 70 whose `on_hit:` carries only damage and sounds. Commit `c86dbd5fa` moved hit3 *out of* `dash_strike.on_hit` (`targets: {type: target}`) *into* a cast-time cone step at 74 ("hit3 via cast-time play_vfx"), i.e. the workflow preferred the frame-aligned tick over the event.
- **Hit geometry built for the capture layout.** `couture-s2-hit2.yaml` (`orient: world`, `follow: false`) has `chain-*` particles from z -0.4 to -4.9 behind the anchor (a chain back to a caster assumed 5 blocks away) and plumes / shards in fixed world directions; `couture-s2-hit3.yaml` spawns six camera-facing `wo-*` white-out sprites. These read right only from the compare camera with the dummy on the line.
- **The design doc baked the dummy in.** The beat sheet (gate 1 passed r1) writes B6 `主 (0.5, 0, 3.6) -> (5.4, 0, 1.2)`, B7 `敵 (5.4, 0.0, 1.0)` and B8 `(5.4, 0, 1.2) -> (0.3, 0.4, 3.9)` as coordinates [I]; `case.json` records the capture as `DISTANCE=5.4` with the reason "keeps the dummy inside the 7-block cone of every hit". The enemy position became a constant at the design stage and the capture was tuned so the constant held.
- **Capture-only verification with a static dummy.** vfx-shot's target is a husk with movement_speed 0 / follow_range 0, facing the caster, at `DISTANCE`; no view moves it, puts it off-axis or removes it. Six rounds, two fresh reviewer FAILs and the round gate all judged frames at ticks 9 / 41 / 72 from that scene; no artefact asked what happens on a miss.
- **Gates rewarded the frame, not the behaviour.** round-gate "Trace" requires `cast+N` contact ticks, "Compare" wants the sheet at those ticks, the reviewer's rows (Motion / Shape / Timing / Hit) are all judged on the sheet; the "Hit" row ("display and damage agree") was marked ○ because, from the compare camera at `DISTANCE 5.4`, they did agree.

Verdict on the four suspects: capture-only verification with a static target, confirmed; fidelity gates that reward matching the frame at a tick, confirmed; no miss case, confirmed (none in any doc, note, gate or capture); VFX timeline tick-scheduled rather than event-driven, confirmed, and the one event-driven binding that existed was removed by hand to fit the frame.
