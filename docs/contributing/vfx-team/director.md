# Director knowledge base

Reference knowledge (2026-10-27): the skill has ONE author who builds every piece; this page is an accumulated knowledge base the author consults, not a role with a territory. Process: [README.md](README.md). Historical Learnings entries may name old roles and numeric targets; self-authored quality numbers are never acceptance evidence (tool-measured facts are mandatory, see "Ground truth" below).

Skill author agent: `.claude/agents/limbus-director.md` (single end-to-end author). Team overview: [README.md](README.md).

> Reference frames are native resolution (<= 1080p, lossless PNG). Judge rotation, contact and blade detail from full-resolution single frames or zoomed crops, never from downscaled contact sheets or strips alone (see [beat-sheet.md](beat-sheet.md) "Reference frame quality").

## What the director produces

the object inventory `tools/skill-compare/data/<case>/inventory.md` ([beat-sheet.md](beat-sheet.md) "Inventory": every object of the reference with count, colour, shape, motion, anchor, medium and engine construction, written from the whole reference at normal speed before any frame is stepped) with its independent inventory diff `inventory-check.md`, the beat sheet `beat-sheet.md` (8 columns, swings counted, rotation direction from the camera and from above, every beat pointing at its inventory rows), `beats` (tick mirror) in `case.json`, the structure commit (one object per inventory row, zero sprites, `make skill-3d` orbit + top stills), the skill YAML, the composite VFX, the capture and its publication, and per build round the round note ([round-template.md](round-template.md): inventory diff first, compare sheet from the API, trace numbers, framing, fidelity, regression) that `make round-gate` checks. Then it fixes reviewer × rows, inventory rows first.

## Ground truth (from [retro-2026-10-27.md](retro-2026-10-27.md))

Two S3 rounds were lost to a +1 tick "fix" made from a mislabelled capture, and three dash-distance readings (3.3 / 5.4 / 6.5) were all eye estimates in a scene whose enemy stood at the tool default distance. Rules:

- **Timing**: the server trace is the only witness. `ASTERA_VFX_TRACE=1` (set in `tools/vfx-shot/compose.override.yml`) logs `[vfx-trace] play <vfx> tick=` and `[vfx-trace] spawn <vfx> <element> age= planSpawn= tick=`; read it with `docker logs` of the capture stack (keep it with `SHOT_KEEP=1`, the stack is removed on exit otherwise). Quote `cast+N` for every `vfx_id` in the round note. Frame labels (`framestamp`, sheet tick chips) are a second witness; when they disagree with the trace, the capture tooling is wrong, not the content. Never shift content by a tick to match a label.
- **Travel**: `[vfx-trace] pos ... caster=` gives the caster position per tick; travel = difference, in blocks. Never read it from pixels.
- **Scene**: side / top / compare captures place the enemy at `DISTANCE` (default 4). Pass the case intent distance (S3: 7.2) and state it in the round note; `dash_strike` stops at `distance - stop_before_target`, so a wrong scene changes the motion itself.
- **Framing**: a review capture is valid only when `framing[].ratioToRef` of the compare API (caster height % of the frame, analytic from the capture camera + trace vs the annotated `ref_caster_height_pct` in case.json) is within 0.8x-1.25x; annotate the ref per camera cut for a new case. Reframe with `FOV_DOLLY` / distance instead of judging a 1/40-frame caster.
- **Tool fidelity**: before judging an effect in a capture or preview, know whether that tool can show it. Spectator clients render no `send_title` / `screen_flash`; skill-preview draws world-space fx full-screen and does not draw ring tiles; `caster` view has F1 on (no first-person hand). Write one line per mechanic type in the round note; "not shown" effects go to gate 2 in game.
- **Regression**: every round compares with the previous published revision at the same ticks and camera (`ref | this | previous`). Look at your own sheet before the reviewer does. `weaker than previous: none` or the round is not finished.
- **Sibling art language**: every round sheet also carries a row of the best accepted frames of the sibling skills of the same weapon / character (round note "Sibling"), and the six art-language lines (`stroke/fill`, `bands`, `taper`, `core line`, `glow`, `outline`, table in [reviewer.md](reviewer.md) "Art language") must read `same` against that sibling. Bastion S3 drifted from S2's layered thin tapered crescent strokes with glow into flat opaque comic stamps (fat plates, black-outlined filled starburst, white blobs) while passing every row: build new elements by copying the sibling's sprite recipe (band order, taper, glow) first, then shape them to the beat.
- **Coverage is not quality**: the compare API's `fx_pct` / `mean_luma` are a floor and a regression signal, never a target. Our own gates pushed S3 toward bigger opaque fills ("never weaker" read as "never less coverage"); a coverage rise obtained with opaque flat fills is a regression, and `fx_pct` never appears in a round note as an improvement.
- **Right medium per element** (founder, S3 rebuild, corrected the same day: 「2Dを使うのに適している場面(カスタム「パーティクル」と斬撃)だよねって話。斬撃自体を2DTextDisplayで作るのはいい」「TextDisplayにするならプレイヤーに常に向く奴はあんまり作っちゃいけない、大きすぎるとチープ具合がバレる。光の柱が特にひどい」): 2D TextDisplay sprites are the right medium for slash arcs on the real swing plane (`billboard: fixed`, euler from the blade path, vfx.md r7 recipe) and for small custom particles; the wrong medium for pillars / beams, plates, filled starbursts, big flat fills, screen overlays and large `billboard: center` stamps (S3 r7: 98 sprite elements, 32 of them center; S2 built its solids as BlockDisplay). Pillars and beams = particle columns plus thin BlockDisplay bars, never one big sprite. `billboard: center` is disallowed by default (it always faces the player; a big one shows its cheapness): each one needs a reason in the round note's per-element medium table, and the gate caps them (10 / 30 % of display elements). Sprites over ~2 blocks need their size and reason in the table (a slash arc on the swing plane may be larger). Before publishing, look at `side` at every key tick: any of those misuses that reads as a pasted sticker is rebuilt in the right medium, not tuned.
- **Hits are events, not ticks** (couture-rapier-s2 r6, founder: 「テストのための実装感がぬぐえない」). `DISTANCE` is a capture parameter, never a design constant: a beat sheet that writes `敵 (5.4, 0, 1.0)` as a coordinate, a thrown weapon flying on caster-local keyframes to that z, a "stuck" model at a caster-local offset, or a hit effect at `delay_ticks` with a cone targeter are all built for the dummy, not the enemy. Thrown weapons are `projectile` mechanics (`visual.display` carries the model; `hit_entity:` / `hit_block:` / `miss:` / `expire:` carry the effects); stuck / linked objects and hit flashes spawn from `on_hit:` / `hit_entity:` anchored to the hit entity (`targets: {type: target}`); a melee hit effect that cannot be an event is at least co-timed with the `damage` that uses the same targeter. `make skill-lint` check 5 fails the fakes before any capture; the round note's Gameplay section carries `miss case:` and `moving target:` from captures with the dummy out of reach / off-axis (or the vfx-shot request they wait on).
- **Respectful remake, not a replica** (founder: 「再現に夢中になりすぎて、普通に武器の挙動として弱いとかっていうのは避けたい。飛び道具が上手く飛ばない、発生フレームが遅すぎる、範囲が狭すぎるetc…」「特に飛び道具の挙動をLimbusのほうに完全にフレームや飛び方も合わせようとするとかなり変になる」): the reference sets the identity (pose silhouettes, key beats, big forms, mood); the skill must play well in Minecraft PvP / PvE. Compress timing (first hit within ~8-10 ticks for a normal skill; a slow heavy says why), enlarge hit areas and ranges for play, give projectiles Minecraft-sensible 3D flight (speed, gravity or straight, 3D hitbox, max range) and never the reference's frame timing or 2D path. Where fidelity and gameplay conflict, gameplay wins and the round note's "Gameplay" section records the deviation; the reviewer weighs those rows like the visual ones.
- **Particles are designed, not copied** (founder: 「パーティクルはLimbusのほうに似せずに、LLM側のセンスで…コンセプトから自ら考えたほうが良いものができる。あとはアニメーションや武器の先端等に連動させて行う程度で」): the reference governs pose, timing, camera and the large forms only. Particles are free design from the skill concept: choose the type, emission point, count, speed, spread, lifetime and colour for good looks in Minecraft, without copying Limbus frames, and anchor them to the animation and the weapon (tip / edge trail along the rig path, the contact point, the dash vector, the landing). Write the `particle concept:` lines of the round note (per beat: intent, anchor, params) before tuning any emitter; the reviewer judges looks and motion linkage, not resemblance.
- **Pose is a reviewed row**: the rig pose must read in both `compare` and `side` (wind-up lean, thrust reach, slam crouch). State the ticks in "Medium" `pose:`; the reviewer marks it.
- **Framing**: `ratioToRef` 0.8x-1.25x (tightened from 0.5x-2x after S3 was rejected as 「近すぎる」 inside the old band).

## Verified facts and where they live

### Procedure and acceptance
- Procedure, acceptance table, status vocabulary (未分析 / 実装済・未検証 / 検証不合格 / 再現合格 / 独自設計・動作確認済): [limbus-skill-reproduction.md](../limbus-skill-reproduction.md) sections 1-7. The 2026-10-23 founder instruction removed the "max 4 iterations" stop: iterate until the match is shown with evidence. Workflow v4 (README process table; checklist in [beat-sheet.md](beat-sheet.md) "Starting a new weapon under v4"): inventory from the whole reference -> independent inventory by a fresh agent and the diff -> beat sheet -> founder gate 1 (inventory + diff + sheet with top-down / side / front stick diagram, on skill-compare) -> structure commit (one object per inventory row, zero sprites, `make skill-3d` stills) -> visuals -> capture (side, front, back, top-down, 45 deg high, eye height) -> fresh reviewer: inventory diff first, then every sheet row ○/× -> founder gate 2 in game. Never build before the inventory diff is clean and gate 1 is approved; never tune a visual before every inventory row has its object. A skill is done only when the reviewer's inventory diff is ok and every row ○ AND the founder approves in game; the director records the approval (date, published revision) in the weapon REPORT.
- Do not change the target to fit the implementation; `intent` is an interpretation of the video, not proof.
- Observation: read the reference first as a list of objects (count, colour, anchor, medium: watch the whole clip 3 times at normal speed, no frame stepping until a row needs confirming), then as motion, never as a pose list. Count the swings, write the direction of every smear from successive frames, and put the rotation direction in both camera and top-down terms. Measure sizes against character height; separate camera change from object scaling. A measurable surface (ticks, coordinates, angles) that matches is not a match when the object count or anchor is wrong (couture-rapier S2 shears).
- Priority: inventory (count, anchor) > motion > colour and shape > detail. A detail round before the inventory passes is wasted.
- Lint is a preflight only (held-weapon count from the inventory, grip from the item data, feet on the floor, display budget, event-bound hits, inventory models, no capture-distance bake; [skill-lint.md](skill-lint.md)); a lint pass proves nothing about the motion.

### Tools
- `make skill-compare-case CASE=<id> VIDEO=<youtubeId> START=<s> END=<s> SKILL=<skill> [WEAPON=<item-id>]` creates `case.json`, an `intent.json` template and native-fps frames. Schema example: `tools/skill-compare/data/zwei-s3/intent.json`.
- `make skill-brief CASE=<id> [VIEW=compare] [AT="t:label"]` writes `build/brief/<case>/sheet-NN.png` + `manifest.json` (reference | intent | in-game per row). Non-empty `warnings` in the manifest means do not use the image as evidence. See [skill-compare README](../../../tools/skill-compare/README.md).
- `make skill-pairs CASE=<id> AT="19.7:hit1 20.9:hit2"` (absolute video seconds).
- Capture: `SKILL=<skill> ITEM=<item-id> VIEW=compare CASE=<case> make vfx-shot` (torn down on exit; with `SHOT_KEEP=1` the stack stays warm for `SHOT_KEEP_TTL` and content / pack / jar changes are synced automatically, ~30 s when only content moved; `make vfx-shot-up` / `-status` / `-down` / `-reap`; `BEAT_TICKS=case` for beat frames only; `meta.json` `tickStamp` summarises the per-frame tick stamp). Always give `ITEM` explicitly (especially `-ego`). Details, views, FOV rules, slots: [vfx-shot README](../../../tools/vfx-shot/README.md).
- Publish after quality review: `node tools/skill-compare/scripts/publish-capture.mjs build/vfx-shot/<skill>/compare <published-root> <skill> compare`. Requires matching skill/view, a contiguous PNG sequence, and no `captureQuality.invalid`. Once published, the server never falls back to the mutable working directory.
- Round-2 captures need matching `referenceRevision` and `contentRevision` in case and `meta.json`; changed content requires recapture.
- After ANY rig re-key or skill timing change (bastion S1): `bash tools/item-art/scripts/bastion_s1_regen.sh` runs the rig-derived chain in order (ring sprites `hring ring` -> `bastion_s1_fx.py` -> `bastion_s1_particles.py` -> `:tools:item-art:run`) and restores the unrelated sprite / preview / `.pyc` re-encodes. Commit its output BEFORE capturing; specialists' own regenerations go stale the moment the animator re-keys after them.
- `--force` capture does not verify cooldown, resources, or key bindings. Check S1/S2/S3/DEF/Q normal input separately.
- Reference weapons are the ADR-0230 roster (10 weapons): [0230-persona-weapon-roster](../../adr/0230-persona-weapon-roster.md). Weapon-level REPORT files live in `tools/skill-compare/data/<weapon>-REPORT.md`.

### Skill YAML
- Reference: [add-a-skill-yaml.md](../add-a-skill-yaml.md). Dash/combo mechanics (`dash_strike`, `hit_stop`, `propel`, `snapshot_pose`, `archetype: sequence`) in its last section.
- Pitfalls from section 4 of the procedure: separate `random` per damage/spark/stun picks different enemies; single-target melee uses `cone.max_count: 1`; `nearby_entities.max_count` excludes the caster; particle `drag` is the fraction lost (0.95 loses 95%); `billboard: center` boards must not also get the caster yaw.
- Display budget: <= 60 simultaneous Displays per cast (E2E measured), <= 300 particles per tick, effect <= 60 ticks (finishers <= 100). Count delayed instances, rig trails, freeze extension.
- Hit VFX and damage on the same tick and same point, also after knockback/movement.

### Composite VFX
- Choreography file `content/vfx/<id>.yaml` (ADR-0220). Rig with `follow: false` for weapon swings. `play_vfx` `freeze: [{at, ticks}]` stops rigs that set `respect_hit_stop: true`.
- Layering order from [vfx-art-direction.md](../vfx-art-direction.md) section 4: telegraph -> motion -> hit -> afterglow. Core opaque (Sakurai), glow as accent, dark mixed with bright.

## Comparing rounds: use the comparison API, not hand-made crops

Reviewers and directors MUST compare with `GET /api/cases/<id>/compare?ticks=..&views=ref,side,top,preview-side&rounds=current,prev` and
`.../sheet.png` (same params; `http://127.0.0.1:8097`), never with hand-cropped or PIL contact sheets. The API aligns reference and in-game frames by the
case's tick mapping, labels revision/sha/age/failure on every row, and returns fx coverage, brightness and regression flags (and caster travel /
final gap when the capture has `trace.log`). Quote its numbers and sheet URL in the report; a `regression: true` is a finding to route, not to ignore.
See [skill-compare README](../../../tools/skill-compare/README.md) "Comparison API".
Look at edges on native crops before handing a round over: add `&zoom=native&crop=x0,y0,x1,y1` (fractions of the frame, same region for ref and in-game; `crop=auto` follows caster + fx) to the sheet URL. Capture side/top with a `RES=` high enough to match the 1080p reference, otherwise the reviewer cannot judge edges.

## Runtime facts and pitfalls (confirmed)

- Font sprites render only up to 256 px; 512 px fails. (Commit `ee976100` on `wp/pilot-bastion-s1`: "512px sprites do not render in the real client (font page limit); 256px with 2x2 supersampled edges is the working maximum.")
- A TextDisplay with `fixed` billboard laid horizontal does not render in the real client (it does in vfx-preview). Never trust vfx-preview alone for horizontal text boards.
- Hit VFX must peak on the damage tick. Verify in the capture frame at the hit tick.
- Only one weapon may be **held** during a skill unless the inventory says otherwise: mechanic `hide_held_item` (schema `content/schemas/skill.schema.json`; used by `content/skills/bastion-greatsword-s2.yaml`, `-ego.yaml`). Every other inventory row (thrown blade, stuck needle, split part) is its own object (projectile sub-skill with a model, anchored element) and may be visible at the same time.
- Rejected looks: crescents built from uniform boxes (hoops); fire made of squares (cubes); poles for jets; a static ring standing like a wall; separate floating sprites instead of one continuous sweep; a pixel-art staircase at close-up; black `#`-like smoke glyphs.
- Machine load causes 2-6 tick (sometimes ~20) capture lag. Reject frozen or magenta-cube captures (`captureQuality.invalid`). Repeated idle frames alone do not prove a stall; `maxRepeatedFrames >= 15` needs visual confirmation.
- Software GL renders ~35 fps at 1280x720, so +-1 tick is normal; do not tune per-hit offsets to hide sync drift.
- Runtime applies 10% / x2 random crits (`CombatWiring`, procedure section 5), so total HP loss does not count hits; count hits from frames or events.
- Machine/slot discipline: one capture runs at a time on the whole host (`/tmp/claude-1000/astera-vfx-shot.lock`, `SHOT_SLOTS=1`); others queue. A run tears its stack down when it exits unless `SHOT_KEEP=1`, and a kept stack lives at most `SHOT_KEEP_TTL` (45 min) or until another checkout needs the slot. Never use the e2e flock for captures. See the "Real-client capture" section of [README.md](README.md).
